# Settle — what works and what doesn't

Live: **https://settleupi.vercel.app** · last checked 25 Oct 2026, 12:40 IST (the 28 Oct work and Stuck money, 2 Nov, are in the repository and awaiting deploy).

Legend: ✅ works on the live site · ⚠️ works with a limit · ❌ not working yet

## Working

| Area | Status | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Install on a phone | ✅ | Opens in any browser; **Add to Home screen** (Android Chrome ⋮ menu, iPhone Safari Share) installs it as an app. Full-screen, offline after the first visit. |
| Split any amount | ✅ | Equally, exact amounts, percentages, shares ("pays double"), item by item, or what each had (a meal with no bill: tax and tip shared in proportion); rounding to whole rupees. |
| Restaurant bill, typed in | ✅ | Items, tax, service charge, discount, "what you actually paid", who had what, per-person result to the paisa. |
| Bill check | ✅ | Before splitting: a service charge added by default, GST on it, service tax (gone since 2017), GST on a bill of supply, odd rates, CGST ≠ SGST, totals above the lines, repeated lines. A note for the staff in eight languages; one tap to split without the charges, with Undo. Scanned bills now read each tax line. |
| Groups | ✅ | Trips, flats, couples; running balances; fewest payments to settle up; UPI QR per payment. |
| Chat to split | ⚠️ | **v9.0:** export a WhatsApp group chat (or paste messages) and Settle reads who paid for what on the phone, shows it to be checked, and makes the group with its people, expenses and the payments between them in one tap; the same chat again adds only what is new. Rules, not AI: English and everyday Hinglish; questions, plans, prices, times and phone numbers are left alone; unsure lines are offered un-ticked. **Not yet tried on real exported chats from phones** (the layouts come from WhatsApp’s documented export formats), and sharing the export straight into Settle needs the installed app on Android. |
| A group’s tools | ✅ | **v9.0:** a row under the balance: the trip kitty (who has put in, what’s left, when it runs out), the "Who pays?" wheel (fair or pure luck), who was there when (late joiners taken off earlier expenses in one tap, a stay split by nights), the group’s story (shareable slides), "looks like this trip is done", an expense claim for the office, and a usual split per group. Kitty, dates and the usual split are kept on each phone; what they change (payments, expenses) is shared like everything else. English only. |
| Clear everything · one payment instead of two · Ask Settle | ✅ | **v9.0:** on Home: pay everyone you owe one after another; when you owe one friend and another owes you, one message and one tap clear both; and questions about your own money ("who owes me?", "what did I spend on food in Goa?") answered on the phone. Ask Settle understands a fixed set of questions and says what it understood. |
| Settle Score · shagun book · veg / no-drinks memory | ✅ | **v9.0:** a 300–900 score for how fast you pay back (a game, not a credit score); the family’s gift ledger with "What should I give?" (in backups, CSV export); and people who don’t drink or are vegetarian taken off those items on every itemised bill by themselves (a lexicon of Indian menu lines; unsure items are left alone). |
| Your UPI ID with no typing · contacts · Split this? | ⚠️ | **v9.0:** your number and your app give the ID for PhonePe, BHIM and Amazon Pay (right only if that app is on that number; Google Pay’s can’t be worked out, its QR is read instead); people added from the phone’s contact picker (Android Chrome and the Android app); and, in the Android app, a payment the phone’s alert shows becomes a one-tap split. **Not yet tried on physical phones.** |
| The Board (groups) | ✅ | **v8.3:** a Board tab on every group: leaderboard with points and levels, titles, on-time streaks, a weekly "square by Sunday" challenge with trophies, milestones, your next move, a budget with a pace forecast, today's spending, and a recap to share. Above the expenses: "Add again" for what the group buys repeatedly, and "Added twice?" for a bill two people added. All worked out from the group's own data, so shared groups see the same Board; the budget is kept on each phone. English only for now. |
| Pay at the counter | ⚠️ | **v8.2:** on a bill you saved, scan the shop's UPI QR once; everyone pays the shop their own share from their own phone (one link, or the QR on your screen), one tap into the UPI app for any amount, and the list of who has paid ticks itself. Nobody fronts the bill. Live ticking needs the relay (Upstash on Vercel); without it friends send "I've paid" or you tick them. **Not yet tried on physical phones** (each UPI app's handling of the link, the camera scanner). |
| Live groups | ⚠️ | **Not deployed yet.** Invite friends to a group with one link; they pick their name and join on their own phone, no sign-up. Expenses, payments, members and UPI IDs then stay the same on every phone by themselves (every few seconds while open), end-to-end encrypted: the server keeps one blob per group it can't read. **Needs storage on the live site:** Vercel → Storage → Upstash Redis, connected to the project (sets `KV_REST_API_URL` / `KV_REST_API_TOKEN`), then redeploy. Without it groups still work by link ("Send update" carries the group; opening it merges). Tested as three phones in real browsers and as up to four in unit tests. |
| Get the app | ✅ | `/get`: Android (the browser's Install) and iPhone (Share → Add to Home Screen), a QR on a computer, a note inside WhatsApp's/Instagram's browser. **v9.0:** the Android app (APK) is a download on `/get`, published by GitHub Actions as a release (a debug build: Android asks to allow the install; not yet tried on a phone). There is no Play Store or App Store listing: store releases need a Play Console and an Apple Developer account, so iPhone stays Add to Home Screen. |
| Pay links | ✅ | One link for the group, or a personal one each; each friend taps their name and pays their exact share in their own UPI app (see "Paying a person" below), then replies "I've paid" with the UPI reference or cash, and you tick them off in one tap. No app or account needed. |
| Auto-settle (Android app) | ⚠️ | **v8.1:** a payment you make from a pay sheet records itself when the phone's alert says the money left (not yet tried on a physical phone). When Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, Amazon Pay, MobiKwik or CRED (or, if allowed, the bank's SMS) says money arrived, whoever owed that is ticked off, with Undo; unsure ones wait for a tap. Read on the phone, nothing uploaded. **Not yet on the Play Store, and not yet tried on physical phones:** CI builds a debug APK (Actions → Android → settle-android-debug); the alert wordings come from published soundbox apps and test texts, not from collected notifications. |
| Stuck money | ⚠️ | **Not deployed yet.** Failed payments that took your money and refunds that haven't come, tracked to the day. **v7.1:** tested end to end by a 24-person panel (English + 7 Indian languages, 360–390 px, light/dark), fixed, and re-tested by the same 24 (7.0 → 8.0 overall, 7.3 → 8.3 for Stuck money); part refunds as a dated history, bank/app/account on the form, send-ready letters, DGCA card rule, wallets, multi-message paste, reminders, Home tile/card. Works everywhere: add one in three taps or paste a bank SMS; the RBI's deadline per kind of payment (T+1 / T+5, calendar days, RBI/2019-20/67); the ₹100 a day owed after it; what to do now, with the letter for each step (bank or UPI app → RBI Ombudsman after 30 days; business → National Consumer Helpline → consumer commission; airlines → AirSewa) to copy, share or email; "I've raised it" and "It's back"; in backups. Settle drafts, the user sends. **Android app only:** spotting failed, pending and refund alerts by itself (UPI apps, a few shopping / food / travel apps, and bank SMS if allowed) and ticking a case off when the money comes back. **Not yet tried on physical phones:** the wording of those alerts (from published samples and test texts), the shopping and travel apps' package names, and how the toasts read when an alert arrives. NPCI counts UPI reversals in working days where the circular says calendar days; refund working days skip weekends, not bank holidays. |
| Bank charges | ⚠️ | **Not deployed yet.** **v7.2:** tested end to end by a 24-person panel (English + 7 Indian languages, 360–390 px, light/dark), fixed, and re-tested by 8 of them (6.4 → 7.4 overall, 6.3 → 7.1 for Bank charges), fixed again. Add a bank statement (CSV, .xlsx or the HTML ".xls" banks email; read on the phone, only the charges kept) or paste bank SMS: every charge is checked against the RBI's rules as of 3 Nov 2026 — minimum balance (negative balance, no month's warning, not short or charged more than the shortfall or the bank's own schedule, Basic Savings / scholarship / inoperative / minor's accounts, the PSBs that stopped charging it), ATM free transactions and failed ones, online NEFT, incoming transfers, UPI, passbook/statement, dormant reactivation, duplicates, GST left on a reversal, prepayment on floating loans, card late fees. One yes/no question where an answer settles it. Wrong charges become a claim per bank: the letter, "I've sent it", the RBI Ombudsman after 30 days, money back. Allowed-but-avoidable charges get tips (the Basic Savings switch letter). Late ATM refunds go to Stuck money. **Android app only:** charge SMS and low-balance warnings read as they come. **Not yet tried on real statements:** narrations come from published complaints and court-filed statements for HDFC, ICICI, SBI, Axis, BoB and BOI plus generic patterns, so other banks' wording may need a pattern; PDF statements can't be read yet; banks' own minimum-balance schedules are kept for HDFC, ICICI, Kotak and Axis only. |
| Who's paid? | ✅ | UPI history screenshots (AI, not kept), a bank statement (CSV, Excel, the HTML ".xls" banks email) or pasted bank SMS (both read on the phone) tick off everyone who paid you back, with Undo. Matched on confirmed bank names, UPI ID, name, amount and date; doubtful ones ask; part payments and overpayments recognised; a statement whose balance doesn't add up ticks nothing for you. A statement shared from another app (Android) opens it. |
| To collect | ✅ | Everyone who owes you, the longest wait first, with when you last reminded them. The next reminder opens friendly, then funny, then firm; "give it a day" after a recent one; a note late at night. Paid ticks them off with Undo. Home's balance card opens it when you're only owed. |
| Fair bijli | ✅ | The flat's electricity bill by the tariff's slabs: everyday units by days at home, each higher slab only on whoever used that much, a lost subsidy on whoever crossed the line. Ten tariffs in force on 27 Oct 2026 (BESCOM, MSEDCL, AEML, Delhi, TNPDCL, TGSPDCL, CESC, Torrent, UGVCL, UPPCL) or a flat rate; AC nights or room meters; reconciled to the bill to the paisa. |
| Reminders and share cards | ✅ | Friendly / funny / firm nudges for WhatsApp and SMS, remembered so the next one knows when you last asked; a receipt image for the group chat. |
| Recurring splits, insights, backups | ✅ | Rent and subscriptions reminded monthly; spending by category and month; export / import a backup file. |
| Claim your items | ✅ | One claim link per bill; friends tick what they had on their own phone; replies merge into the split. |
| From SMS | ✅ | Paste or share a bank / UPI alert to split it; OTP messages are refused. Sharing into Settle needs the installed app on Android. |
| Food orders | ✅ | Delivery, platform, packaging, surge and small-order fees kept apart and shared in proportion or equally. |
| Collections (chanda) | ✅ | One `/pool` link for a gift, party or puja; everyone pays by UPI and replies; you tick them off. |
| Udhaar (lend / borrow) | ✅ | Two taps; part payments; reminders. |
| Next round's on you | ✅ | Groups that take turns paying see whose round it is. |
| Trips abroad | ✅ | Spend in another currency, settle in ₹ at that day's ECB rate (cached for offline), your own rate or your bank's total. |
| Rupees by default in India | ✅ | Uses the phone's time zone, so English (US) phones in India still start in ₹. |
| Speed | ✅ | On a budget-phone profile: a 300-expense group opens in 1.6 s (was 7.7 s); Home with two years of data 6.5 s (was 8.5 s); details in [bench/RESULTS.md](bench/RESULTS.md). |
| Security | ✅ | Strict security headers (CSP, HSTS, no framing), signed claim / collection replies, other sites can't call the API, per-visitor rate limits. |

## Not working yet, or limited

| Area | Status | Why / what's needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AI on the live site (scan a bill photo, "Just say it", speak to split, "let AI read" an SMS) | ✅ | The NVIDIA key is on Vercel (25 Oct). Checked live: a restaurant bill photo read in 4.4 s (7 items, ₹2,766 total) and "Dinner 2400 with Riya and Sam" turned into an equal ₹2,400 split. Free plan: 3 AI splits a day per phone. |
| Pro (paid plan) on the live site | ⚠️ | Licence keys are on Vercel, so **Redeem a code** works (`npm run license issue …` makes codes). Selling Pro needs Razorpay keys (`RAZORPAY_KEY_ID`, `RAZORPAY_KEY_SECRET`); until then the Pro page shows the plans without checkout. |
| Indian languages for the screens | ⚠️ | Eight languages (English, Hindi, Bengali, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Gujarati) on every app screen; pick on the first onboarding screen or in You → Language. **Translations not yet reviewed by native speakers**, and the landing page and the privacy / terms text stay in English. |
| Rent & HRA receipts | ✅ | Set up a flat's rent (landlord's UPI ID and PAN, each flatmate's share). One rent link a month: each flatmate pays the landlord their own share directly. HRA rent receipts per flatmate, monthly or quarterly, with the PAN and revenue-stamp rules checked. Receipts print in English; the screens are in English and Hindi (other languages show English for now). |
| Paying a person (NPCI rules) | ⚠️ | **v8.0:** on a phone, Pay leads with "Pay ₹400 with Google Pay" (PhonePe / Paytm / BHIM; remembered): up to ₹2,000 the payee's QR is handed to the app (share sheet; straight to the app in the Android app), above that the UPI ID is copied and the app opened; a direct link is offered as a labelled try. **Not yet tried on physical phones:** whether each app accepts a shared QR picture, and what each does with the direct link. UPI apps refuse a payment to a person that starts from a link or intent (NPCI OC 76C; Consolidated Circular 7.9), so Settle offers what UPI allows: on a computer, the QR (amount filled in, any amount); on a phone, the QR saved to the gallery and uploaded in the UPI app (up to ₹2,000 — UPI's cap for a QR picture to a person) or the UPI ID to copy with the amount; iPhone can open GPay, PhonePe, Paytm or BHIM on their home screen. **Not yet tried on physical phones**: the gallery route and the ₹2,000 cap per app, and the iPhone app schemes. Settle can't move money itself — that needs an NPCI licence. |
| Bank statements in "Who's paid?" | ⚠️ | Read on the phone from CSV, Excel (.xlsx) and the HTML ".xls" banks email; the column and narration formats (HDFC, SBI, ICICI, Axis, Kotak and similar) were built from published layouts and synthetic files, **not yet from real statements**, so an unusual layout may need a fix. Old binary .xls files can't be read — save as CSV or .xlsx. |
| "Install Settle" card | ✅ | On Home after the first split or second visit, and a You row. Android / desktop Chrome use the install prompt; iPhone gets the two Share → Add to Home Screen steps. Hidden once installed; dismissible for 30 days. |
| Paid? Drop the screenshot | ✅ | Drop a GPay / PhonePe / Paytm / BHIM payment screenshot: the AI reads status, amount, payee, UTR and date and matches it to who owes you. Refuses pending or failed payments, payments to someone else and duplicate UTRs. Uses the same AI key, now live. |
| Very long histories on budget phones | ⚠️ | Scrolling Activity to the oldest of 1,500 entries takes ~59 s on the budget-phone profile (was 165 s). |
| Speak to split | ⚠️ | Needs a browser with speech recognition (Chrome, Edge, Safari; not Firefox) and the AI key. |
| Sharing an SMS into Settle | ⚠️ | Android with the installed app only (iPhone doesn't let web apps receive shares). |
| Using one account on two phones | ⚠️ by design | No accounts and no database: data stays on the phone. Move it with a backup file. |
| Automatic deploys | ⚠️ | Deploys run from the command line (`npx vercel deploy --prod`); GitHub isn't connected to Vercel for deploy-on-push yet. |

## Tests (28 Oct 2026)

- Unit and component tests: **5,081 passed**, 0 failed; typecheck and lint clean. (On a busy machine a few slow screen tests can hit the 5-second limit; they pass when run again.)
- End-to-end on the production build, Microsoft Edge as 360 px and 390 px phones in light and dark (364 journeys): every journey for the new work passes — Auto-settle, "Who's paid?" (statement, screenshots, a statement shared in through the service worker), "To collect", part payments, pay links, offline straight after the first visit (red since v5, now fixed), sharing a bill photo in. The 1,500-expense scroll test needs a quiet machine (60 s alone, against a 90 s budget). CI runs the whole suite in four parallel shards.
- The Android app compiles in CI (Actions → Android, debug APK).
